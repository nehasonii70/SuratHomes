The Journey

Step-by-Step Buying Process

1

Set Your Budget

Determine your total purchase budget factoring in down payment (typically 20%), stamp duty (4.9% in Gujarat), registration charges, and GST on under-construction flats. A financial advisor can help structure your investment correctly.

1
2
2

Search & Shortlist

Use SuratHomes to filter by location, BHK, budget, and status. Shortlist 3–5 properties and verify each listing's RERA number on the Gujarat RERA portal before scheduling visits.

3

Legal Due Diligence

Verify the title deed, encumbrance certificate, property tax receipts, and occupancy certificate (OC). Confirm RERA registration is valid and that no pending liabilities exist on the property.

3
4
4

Negotiate & Agree

Negotiate the final price with the seller or builder. Sign a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) and pay 5-10% token amount. Ensure all verbal promises are documented in the agreement.

5

Secure Home Loan

Apply for home loan sanction from banks (SBI, HDFC, ICICI offer best rates for Gujarat properties). Submit income proof, property documents, and get in-principal approval before finalizing payment schedule.

5
6
6

Registration & Handover

Visit the sub-registrar office for the sale deed registration. Pay stamp duty and registration charges. Collect the original registered sale deed and take possession with a detailed inventory checklist.

Stay Protected

Legal Due Diligence Checklist

Before you sign any agreement, ensure the following documents are verified:

Title Deed Verification

Confirm chain of ownership goes back at least 30 years without disputes.

Encumbrance Certificate

Verify no pending mortgages, liens, or liabilities on the property.

RERA Registration

Check project RERA number on Gujarat RERA portal for authentic builder details.

Occupancy Certificate (OC)

Required for ready-to-move-in properties to confirm the building is approved for habitation.

Property Tax Clearance

Ensure all past property tax dues are fully cleared before completing the purchase.

Building Approval Plans

Verify the building plan is sanctioned by the Surat Municipal Corporation (SMC).
