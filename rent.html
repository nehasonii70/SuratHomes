Be a Smart Tenant

Rental Tenant Checklist

Read the Lease Agreement

Review rent escalation clauses, maintenance responsibilities, and exit notice period before signing.

Document Existing Defects

Photograph and email existing damage to the landlord before moving in to avoid disputes during exit.

Verify Utility Bills

Ensure no outstanding electricity, water, or gas bills are pending from previous tenants.

Police Verification

Mandatory in Gujarat. Submit Form C at local police station within 24 hours of moving in.
