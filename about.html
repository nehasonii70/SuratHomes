The Origin

How SuratHomes Began

SuratHomes was conceptualized in 2018 by a team of Surat-based real estate professionals who saw a critical market gap — buyers and renters in Surat were overwhelmed by unverified listings, opaque pricing, and dishonest brokerage practices.

We set out to build something different: a premium platform that combines verified RERA listings, local neighborhood expertise, and a concierge-style agent network — all in one seamless digital experience.

Today, SuratHomes is proud to be the real estate partner of choice for over 1,200 families, hundreds of NRI investors, and Surat's leading builders and developers.

Our Mission

To simplify and dignify real estate transactions in Surat through technology, verified data, and expert human guidance — making property ownership accessible, transparent, and enjoyable for all.

Our Vision

To become Gujarat's most trusted and innovative real estate ecosystem — connecting every buyer, seller, investor, and builder through a platform built on integrity, local knowledge, and premium service.
